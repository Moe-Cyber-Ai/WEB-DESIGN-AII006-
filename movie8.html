CENTRAL INTELLIGENCE

Saving the world takes a little Hart and a big Johnson.

Central Intelligence combines explosive action, comedy, and secret-agent adventure starring Dwayne Johnson and Kevin Hart. The movie follows two former classmates who reunite years later and unexpectedly become involved in a dangerous CIA mission filled with humor, espionage, and nonstop action.

Title of the Movie Central Intelligence
Director Rawson Marshall Thurber
Producer Scott Stuber, Peter Principato, Paul Young, and Michael Fottrell
Leading Actor Dwayne Johnson and Kevin Hart
Leading Actress Danielle Nicolet
Movie Rating PG-13
Brief Description Central Intelligence is an action-comedy movie about a former bullied high school student who grows up to become a dangerous CIA agent. He reconnects with his old classmate, an accountant living a normal life, and pulls him into a world full of secret missions, gun fights, international spies, and hilarious adventures.
