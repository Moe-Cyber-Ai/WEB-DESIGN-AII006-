Observations and Conclusions

The survey results clearly show that online shopping is extremely popular among younger people, especially students and young adults aged 18–25. Most participants reported shopping online at least once a month, while several people shop online weekly.

Amazon was the most popular website because participants believed it offered fast shipping, better prices, and a large variety of products. Fashion websites such as Shein and Trendyol were also popular among younger female participants.

People who did not purchase online said they still browse products regularly but are concerned about payment security, delivery delays, or prefer shopping in physical stores.

Participants with college education or graduate education tended to purchase online more frequently compared to participants with only high school education.

Overall, the survey demonstrates that e-commerce has become a major part of everyday life, especially for students and working adults who value convenience and quick access to products.

Supporting Web Research

  • According to the Pew Internet and American Life Project, internet users increasingly rely on online shopping because of convenience and product variety.
  • eMarketer reports that global e-commerce sales continue to grow every year as more consumers trust online payment systems and mobile shopping applications.
  • ClickZ research highlights that younger consumers are the most active online shoppers, especially through smartphones and social media advertisements.
